>
> Hi Jan,
>
> I am building **API Dependency Sentinel** for teams that maintain integrations with providers such as Stripe, OpenAI, and Twilio. I am not assuming there is a problem in n8n’s implementation; I am testing whether a source-backed way to identify likely consuming code and a reviewer earlier would be useful.
