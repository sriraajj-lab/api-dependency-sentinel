# Venture Signal Hub — Design Directions

## Approach 1: The Signal Room

**Very Brief Intro:** A composed editorial intelligence desk: warm paper, ink-like type, restrained data color, and a persistent research-rail. It should feel like a private briefing assembled for a partner meeting rather than a generic SaaS dashboard.

**Probability:** 0.07

## Approach 2: Neon Due Diligence

**Very Brief Intro:** A deep-night command center with saturated luminous data marks and dense scanning surfaces. It would feel fast and technical, but risks making the research feel like a trading terminal.

**Probability:** 0.03

## Approach 3: The Field Atlas

**Very Brief Intro:** An archival, map-inspired experience that treats startup categories as an evolving territory. Organic color blocks and oversized index labels make discovery feel more exploratory than analytical.

**Probability:** 0.09

# Selected Direction: The Signal Room

## Design Movement

**Contemporary editorial intelligence**: a synthesis of a research journal, a boutique investment memo, and a modern editorial workspace. The UI uses high-contrast serif headlines, quiet technical sans-serif metadata, warm off-white surfaces, and sharp forest-black rules.

## Core Principles

1. **Evidence before decoration:** Every visual element supports scanning, filtering, comparison, or source confidence.
2. **Asymmetric research flow:** The page reads like an annotated briefing with a compact left rail and an expansive, shifting content field.
3. **Measured density:** Information-rich cards and tables live inside generous whitespace so the product feels decisive rather than crowded.
4. **Trust in the details:** Source labels, dates, type categories, and subtle data markers are explicit throughout.

## Color Philosophy

The signature field is **Signal Vermilion**—a warm red-orange used sparingly to mark emerging opportunity and active research. It sits against archival ivory, carbon ink, moss black, and mineral blue-grey. The warm/cool tension gives the hub the feeling of a physical research dossier annotated with a highlighter.

## Layout Paradigm

A **research-rail composition**: a narrow sticky navigation rail becomes an index of the research system, while the main column uses offset cards, split headlines, and horizontal dataset strips. Avoid a centered marketing layout; the information field should feel like an analyst’s desk.

## Signature Elements

1. **Signal stamps:** small outlined capsules for source, theme, and confidence that echo library catalogue labels.
2. **The horizon line:** a vermilion scan line that appears in the hero and reappears as a delimiter for important sections.
3. **Dossier cards:** near-square, high-contrast cards with oversized category numerals, a short thesis, and a source trail.

## Interaction Philosophy

Discovery should feel intentional and quiet. Search narrows the knowledge set immediately. Filters act like research tabs. Cards elevate subtly, reveal an action affordance on hover, and never distract from the reading task.

## Animation

Use short 160–240ms opacity and transform transitions with a custom ease-out. Initial cards enter with a restrained stagger. Search and filter state changes update instantly without theatrical motion. Respect reduced-motion preferences.

## Typography System

**DM Serif Display** provides the thesis-level headlines, with a measured, literary authority. **IBM Plex Sans** carries metadata, UI, and body copy for a technical research-note character. Headings are tight and editorial; metadata is compact, tracked, and uppercase.

## Brand Essence

**Venture Signal Hub is the research cockpit for investors looking for the work behind the AI hype.**

Personality adjectives: **discerning, grounded, forward-looking**.

## Brand Voice

Headlines are decisive and evidence-led; CTAs are invitational but specific. Avoid generic startup language and exaggerated promises.

Example lines:

- “Trace the work becoming software.”
- “From noisy launches to investable patterns.”

## Wordmark & Logo

The logo is a compact **horizon-and-beacon** symbol: a black circular field interrupted by a single vermilion scan line and a small radiant point. It represents the act of finding an actionable signal in a wide field of information. The wordmark uses DM Serif Display, never a default UI typeface.

## Signature Brand Color

**Signal Vermilion — #E84B2F**

## Style Decisions

- **Palette rule:** Signal Vermilion #E84B2F is the only warm accent and is reserved for active research states, horizon lines, key numerals, stamps, and primary actions. Category differentiation comes from labels, rules, and neutral paper tones rather than soft pastels.
- **Dossier rule:** Every primary signal card must visibly include a category label, confidence/source stamp, source trail, and one oversized index or signal numeral so cards read as evidence records, not generic content tiles.
- **Layout rule:** Every major section retains a research-rail or ledger cue—a numbered cut, source label, left annotation, or rule system—so the experience remains one continuous analyst briefing.
